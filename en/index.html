Pullover Coach

An iPhone app that reads one side-view video of your child's pullover on the bar, narrows it down to the one thing to fix first, and shows drills for exactly that.

  • Measures foot position, elbow bend, kick height and hip-to-bar distance on the device
  • Three-step drills for each cause, with reps, where to spot and what to say
  • Export a day-one vs. latest comparison video with faces blurred automatically
  • Videos never leave your iPhone. No account needed
