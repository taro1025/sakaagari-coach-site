Privacy Policy

This policy describes how the iPhone app “Pullover Coach” (the App) handles information.

What we collect

We do not collect any personal information from the App. The App has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

Videos and records

Videos you film or choose, the results of body tracking, your child's name and school year, and practice records are stored only on your device. Body tracking runs on the device using Apple's Vision framework; nothing is sent outside.

Camera and Photos

The camera is used to film the pullover. No audio is recorded. The App asks for add-only access to Photos only when saving a comparison video. When you pick a video from Photos, the App receives only that video.

Purchases

Pro purchases are processed by Apple's App Store. We do not receive payment information.

Half-price offer

If you turn off auto-renewal for the monthly plan, the App offers half price through an App Store promotional offer. The App asks why you turned it off; your answer is used only on the device to adjust the next screen and is never sent. Only when you tap the button to keep Pro at half price does the App send the app, product and offer IDs to our server to receive the signature the App Store requires. Your child's details, videos, your answer and your Apple ID are not sent.

Deleting data

Settings > Delete all videos and analyses removes them. Deleting the App removes all data.

Children

The App is used by parents. Your child's videos and details stay on the device and are never received by us.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page.
